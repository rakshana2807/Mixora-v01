# MIXORA 🧋✨
> **Your Drink. Your Taste. Your MIXORA.**

MIXORA is a modern, premium online boba café web application designed to deliver an interactive beverage customization experience. Customers can explore handcrafted bubble tea recipes, discover drinks personalized to their mood using the Boba Personality Lab, customize their drinks in real time with a live visualizer, and manage their orders through a responsive dashboard.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🧋 Interactive Drink Studio & Customizer
- **Real-Time Visualizer**: Build custom beverages layer-by-layer with live visual feedback.
- **Deep Customization**: Choose cup size, ice level, sweetness percentage, tea bases, popping/tapioca boba, jellies, and cloud foam toppings.
- **Dynamic Price Engine**: Instant pricing calculation that updates as ingredients and toppings are customized.
- **Surprise Me & Recipe Saving**: One-click randomized drink generation and option to save signature custom recipes to user profiles.

### 2. 🧪 Boba Personality Lab (AI Taste Matcher)
- **Interactive 3-Question Quiz**: Discover a signature beverage tailored to your energy level, texture preferences, and daily vibe.
- **Single Signature Drink Recommendation**: Generates a personalized drink card with matching toppings, custom card artwork, and tasting notes.
- **One-Click Add & Customize**: Directly customize or add the recommended drink straight to the cart with celebratory animations.

### 3. 📋 Artisanal Menu & Quick Order
- **Category Filtering**: Explore categorized collections including Milk Tea, Brown Sugar Boba, Matcha, Fruit Tea, Smoothies, and Milkshakes.
- **Real-Time Search**: Instant search by drink title or ingredient.
- **Quick Order**: Fast one-click ordering with default recipe settings.

### 4. 🛍️ Cart & Seamless Checkout
- **Slide-out & Dedicated Cart**: Persistent shopping cart with real-time item counter and price breakdown.
- **Order Fulfilment Options**: Support for Pickup and Delivery options.
- **Instant Checkout**: Address input, delivery notes, and instant simulated order creation.

### 5. 👤 Global Customer Profile
- **Synchronized User Details**: Persistent profile management with customer details, saved addresses, drink preferences, and past orders.
- **Saved Recipes & Quick Reorder**: View previously saved personal drink creations and reorder them in one click.

### 6. 📊 Shop Owner Admin Dashboard
- **Unified Order Management**: A single, consolidated orders table with real-time status filter tabs (*All, New, Confirmed, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, Completed, Cancelled / Rejected*) and dynamic count badges.
- **Live KPI Summary**: Real-time KPI cards tracking Today's Orders, New Orders, Orders in Prep, Ready for Pickup, and Live Revenue.
- **Kitchen Ticket Printing**: One-click printable kitchen prep tickets for staff.
- **Static, Collapsible Sidebar**: Sticky non-scrolling sidebar with desktop collapse/expand toggle and mobile drawer support.
